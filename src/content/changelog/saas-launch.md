### Account & cloud save

- Supabase Auth on login, signup, and password reset
- Novels, chapters, scenes, and Codex entries persist per account
- Autosave in Write mode

### Plans & billing

- Free, Pro, and Studio tiers on the homepage
- Stripe checkout and webhooks (configure env vars in Netlify)

### AI features

- Story-aware **Chat** and manuscript **Review** (metered by plan credits)

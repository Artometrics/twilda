## Plans

Twilda offers **Free**, **Pro**, and **Studio** plans. See the [pricing section](/#pricing-section) on the homepage for feature comparisons.

- **Pro** — self-serve Stripe checkout from Account → Billing.
- **Studio** — contact us for onboarding; not available as one-click checkout.

## AI credits

Chat and Review each use one AI credit per successful request. If an AI call fails, the credit is refunded. Your remaining credits appear in the novels workspace sidebar and on your account page.

| Plan | Monthly credits |
|------|-----------------|
| Free | 5 |
| Pro | 50 |
| Studio | 200 |

## Managing billing

1. Go to [Account → Billing](/account/billing/).
2. Click **Upgrade to Pro** for self-serve checkout, or **Contact for Studio**.
3. Complete checkout in Stripe (Pro).
4. Return to Twilda — your plan and credits update automatically.

If checkout is unavailable in your environment, contact us via the [contact form](/forms/contact/).

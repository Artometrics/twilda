Welcome to **Twilda** — Artometrics' novel-writing workspace with Atlas, GOTHA, and My Museum.

1. **Sign up** at `/forms/signup/` (free plans land on `/novels/?welcome=1` with a short checklist).
2. Open **Novels** — we seed three classic starters (**Gatsby**, **Trinity**, **Cardinal**). Starters do not count against the Free novel limit.
3. Use **Plan** for your outline, **Write** for drafting, and **Codex** for characters and lore.
4. Try **Chat** or **Review** when you need AI feedback (uses credits when the model responds).
5. Optional: explore **Atlas** (history map + Met Open Access), save artifacts to **My Museum**, and try a **GOTHA** sample family tree.

Need help? Visit **Help Center** or contact us via the contact form.
